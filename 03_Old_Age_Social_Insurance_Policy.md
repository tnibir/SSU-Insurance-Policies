## 1.0 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. Bangladesh is on the threshold of a profound demographic transition. By 2040, the national population aged 60 and above will exceed 30 million individuals—more than doubling from approximately 14 million today. While Bangladesh has expanded non-contributory cash transfers such as the **Old Age Allowance (Boyshko Bhata)** covering 62 lakh citizens at Tk 700 per month, and launched the **Universal Pension Scheme (UPS)** in 2023, the country lacks a **statutory, mandatory contributory old-age social insurance system** for the formal private and broad working-age economy.
2. The current retirement landscape suffers from severe structural fractures:
   - **Civil Service Pension Distortion:** Approximately 1.5 million public sector employees receive generous, tax-financed defined-benefit pensions consuming over 8% of the national budget.
   - **Voluntary Under-Participation in Private Pensions:** Private employers are not legally mandated to co-contribute to worker retirement. Consequently, voluntary uptake under the UPS *Progoti* scheme remains minimal.
   - **Safety Net Inadequacy:** The non-contributory Old Age Allowance of Tk 700/month is less than 5% of average formal industrial earnings, failing to prevent elderly poverty.
3. This policy establishes the **National Old-Age Social Insurance Scheme (NOASIS)** under the **National Social Security Strategy (NSSS)**, fully harmonized with **ILO Convention No. 102 (Part V: Old-Age Benefit)** and **ILO Convention No. 128 (Invalidity, Old-Age and Survivors' Benefits Convention, 1967)**.
4. NOASIS introduces a sustainable **Multi-Pillar Pension Architecture**:
   - **Pillar 0 (Universal Non-Contributory Safety Net):** Reformed, inflation-indexed Old Age Allowance for destitute and low-income elderly.
   - **Pillar 1 (Mandatory Contributory Social Insurance):** Compulsory, earnings-related, shared employer-employee contributions for all formal private enterprises and registered establishments.
   - **Pillar 2 (Subsidized Semi-Formal / Informal Window - Enhanced Samata/Surakkha):** Matching state subsidies for low-income informal workers.
   - **Pillar 3 (Voluntary Supplementary Savings):** Tax-advantaged private voluntary pension tiers.
5. In accordance with national drafting standards where demographic census micro-simulations are ongoing, this policy document provides a complete textual and institutional blueprint. Every calculative field (contribution rate %, retirement age, vesting periods, replacement rates, annuity conversion factors, and reserve accumulation schedules) is demarcated with **standardized bracketed placeholders** containing precise actuarial guidance.
6. A comprehensive audit of existing non-contributory assistance programs in Bangladesh is presented in the final section.

## 2.0 POLICY CONTEXT, LEGAL BASIS & STRATEGIC RATIONALE

### 2.1 Constitutional Anchor
This policy directly operationalizes **Article 15(d) of the Constitution of the People's Republic of Bangladesh**, which mandates:
> *"The right to public assistance in cases of undeserved want arising from... old age..."*

### 2.2 National Strategic Alignment
1. **National Social Security Strategy (NSSS):** Identifies old-age income security as a core lifecycle pillar and mandates the consolidation of fragmented transfers into a comprehensive national pension framework.
2. **Universal Pension Management Act, 2023:** Establishes the National Pension Authority (NPA). This policy provides the necessary regulatory framework to transition the NPA’s private-sector scheme (*Progoti*) from a voluntary model to a **mandatory statutory employment obligation**.

### 2.3 The Looming Elderly Crisis in Bangladesh
- **Rapid Population Ageing:** The elderly dependency ratio (ratio of persons aged 65+ per 100 working-age persons) will rise from 8.5% in 2026 to over 22.0% by 2050.
- **Collapse of the Traditional Joint Family Safety Net:** Urbanization, industrial migration, and nuclear family structures have eroded the informal family safety net. Over 68% of elderly citizens report financial insecurity or dependence on erratic remittances.
- **Absence of Mandatory Enterprise Severance Pooling:** Most private enterprises maintain unreserved, book-entry gratuity accounts that often default when companies restructure or enter bankruptcy.

Low-income basic pension funded via taxes. | Earnings-related with a redistributive formula combining individual lifetime earnings and average national earnings; target 40% replacement at 40 years. | Pensions adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI). ## 5.0 BENEFIT STRUCTURE, FORMULAS & ANNUITIZATION

### 5.1 Benefit Modality: Hybrid Defined Benefit / Notional Contributory Pension
NOASIS guarantees a **monthly lifetime pension** calculated to ensure both earnings-related income replacement and redistributive social protection for lower-wage workers.

```
  =====================================================================================
  STATUTORY MONTHLY PENSION FORMULA:
  
    Monthly Pension = Minimum_Pension_Floor
                      + [ Accrual_Rate × Qualifying_Years × Average_Revalued_Wage ]
  =====================================================================================
```

   - No worker who completes the statutory vesting period (10 years) shall receive a monthly pension lower than `[PLACEHOLDER: Minimum Guaranteed Pension Floor Tk - Instruction: Recommended Tk 4,500 to Tk 6,000 per month; benchmarked to 150% of the upper poverty line or 50% of national minimum wage]`.
4. **Survivors' and Nominee Pension:**

   - Guaranteed minimum payout: If the retiree dies before receiving benefits for `[PLACEHOLDER: Guaranteed Annuity Period - Instruction: Standard 15 years / 180 monthly payments]`, the balance of the remaining guaranteed period is disbursed to named nominees.

### 5.2 Annual Pension Indexation
To protect elderly retirees against purchasing power erosion, monthly pensions are indexed annually on the 1st of July according to the **National Pension Indexation Formula**:

$$\text{Annual Indexation Adjustment \%} = \alpha \times \Delta\text{CPI} + (1 - \alpha) \times \Delta\text{WRI}$$

Where:
- $\Delta\text{CPI}$ is the national Consumer Price Index inflation rate (BBS);
- $\Delta\text{WRI}$ is the national nominal Wage Rate Index growth rate (BBS);
- $\alpha$ is the weighting coefficient `[PLACEHOLDER: Indexation Weighting - Instruction: Recommended 0.50 (equal 50/50 weighting of price inflation and real wage growth)]`.

### 6.3 Pension Reserve Fund Governance & Sovereign Investment Mandate
The multi-trillion Taka reserve fund generated under NOASIS shall be managed under strict statutory fiduciary guidelines:
- **Independent Board of Trustees:** Chaired by the Governor of Bangladesh Bank, featuring prominent economists, actuarial fellows, and tripartite representatives.
- **Permissible Investment Asset Classes:**
  - Minimum `[60%]` in Government Treasury Bonds (5Y, 10Y, 15Y, 20Y);
  - Maximum `[25%]` in AAA-rated national infrastructure debentures (power, ports, Padma Bridge / Metrorail bonds);
  - Maximum `[15%]` in blue-chip domestic capital market equities and authorized real estate securities.
- **Prohibition on Speculative Lending:** Direct uncollateralized lending to private commercial enterprises or politically directed projects is strictly prohibited.

### 7.1 National Pension Authority (NPA) Operational Linkage
The **National Pension Authority (NPA)** under the Finance Division shall act as the apex operational executing agency, working in close coordination with the MoLE Social Security Unit:
- **Universal Pension ID (UPID):** Every citizen receives a permanent, non-transferable 12-digit UPID linked to their biometric National ID (NID).
- **Portability Across Employers:** A worker retains the same UPID when switching jobs between companies, sectors, or transitioning between formal employment and informal self-employment.
- **Automated Direct-Debit & Collection:** Contributions automatically collected via the Bangladesh Automated Clearing House (BACH), RTGS, and mobile financial services on the 10th of each calendar month.

### 8.1 Detailed Inventory of Existing Schemes in Bangladesh

#### 1. Old Age Allowance (Boyshko Bhata)
- **Legal & Policy Anchor:** Non-contributory cash transfer under the Ministry of Social Welfare (MoSW) / Department of Social Services (DSS); anchored in Article 15(d) of the Constitution and NSSS Elderly Pillar.
- **Official FY2026-27 Figures (Finance Division Social Security Budget Report):**
  - **Monthly Allowance:** **Tk 700 per month** (disbursed quarterly via mobile wallets).
  - **Beneficiary Target:** **62 lakh (6.20 million)** destitute elderly citizens (men aged 65+, women aged 62+).
  - **Annual Budget Allocation:** **Tk 5,239.25 crore**.
- **Structural Deficiencies:**
  - *Severe Benefit Inadequacy:* Tk 700/month covers less than 15% of the minimum cost of food and basic medicines for an elderly person.
  - *Exclusion Errors:* Due to strict budgetary quotas, millions of vulnerable near-poor seniors are excluded.
  - *Fiscal Saturation:* Expanding the Old Age Allowance to keep pace with rapid population ageing would place an unsustainable strain on general tax revenues.

#### 2. Allowance for Widowed and Husband-Deserted Destitute Women
- **Legal & Policy Anchor:** Non-contributory cash transfer administered by MoSW/DSS under the *Implementation Manual for Allowances to Husband-Deserted and Widowed Women, 2024*.
- **Official FY2026-27 Figures:**
  - **Monthly Allowance:** **Tk 700 per month**.
  - **Beneficiary Target:** **30 lakh (3.00 million)** vulnerable women.
  - **Annual Budget Allocation:** **Tk 2,535.12 crore**.
- **Structural Deficiencies:** Operates as a distress relief transfer; provides no replacement of former earnings and offers no survivor annuity guarantees.

#### 3. The Universal Pension Scheme (UPS) — Operational Since August 2023
- **Legal Anchor:** Universal Pension Management Act, 2023; administered by the National Pension Authority (NPA).
- **Existing Scheme Windows & Contribution Schedules:**
  - **Progoti Scheme:** For private formal enterprise employees. Monthly contribution tiers: **Tk 2,000 / Tk 3,000 / Tk 5,000 / Tk 10,000**. Employer may contribute 50%, but employer participation is **voluntary**.
  - **Probash Scheme:** For expatriate Bangladeshi migrant workers. Monthly contribution tiers: **Tk 2,000 / Tk 5,000 / Tk 7,500 / Tk 10,000**.
  - **Surakkha Scheme:** For informal economy workers (farmers, day-laborers, gig workers). Monthly contribution tiers: **Tk 1,000 / Tk 2,000 / Tk 3,000 / Tk 5,000**.
  - **Samata Scheme:** For citizens living below the extreme poverty line. Monthly contribution: **Tk 1,000 total**, split **Tk 500 subscriber + Tk 500 government matching subsidy**.
- **Official FY2026-27 Public Budget Allocation (NPA Samata Scheme):**
  - **Tk 10.00 crore** allocated to cover the matching grant component for the extreme poor.
- **Structural Limitations of the Current UPS:**
  - *Voluntary Flaw:* Because *Progoti* is voluntary, less than 2% of private enterprises have registered their workforces. Without a statutory mandate, employers choose not to contribute.
  - *Fixed Nominal Tiering vs. Payroll %:* Contribution tiers are fixed nominal Taka amounts rather than a percentage of gross wages, which leads to real-value depreciation over long inflationary horizons.

#### 4. Government Non-Contributory Civil Service Pension
- **Legal Character:** Non-contributory defined benefit pension for government officials under the Public Service Act and Civil Service Regulations.
- **Benefit Level:** Up to 90% of last drawn basic salary, plus 50% medical allowance and annual festival bonuses, indexed to civil service pay scales.
- **Fiscal Impact:** Consumes over **Tk 30,000 crore annually** (exceeding 8% of national revenue budget) while covering fewer than 5% of the national elderly population.

### 8.3 Strategic Imperative for Mandatory Enactment
Relying entirely on general taxation to support an ageing population will inevitably trigger fiscal insolvency. Conversely, relying on purely voluntary savings fails due to short-term individual behavior and employer evasion. Enacting the **National Old-Age Social Insurance Scheme (NOASIS)** creates a self-financing, pre-funded pension system that guarantees dignity in old age for every Bangladeshi worker while generating billions of dollars in long-term domestic capital for national development.
